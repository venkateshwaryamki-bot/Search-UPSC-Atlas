## Full description (both stores; Play max 4000, App Store max 4000)
Search UPSC Atlas is an interactive map of India made for UPSC Civil Services preparation. Tap any part of the map to get exam-ready facts, then test yourself with quizzes you answer on the map.

EXPLORE
• All 28 states and 8 Union Territories, coloured by Zonal Council, each with a fact card: capital, international borders, coastline, Fifth and Sixth Schedule status, rivers, ranges, passes, protected areas, crops, dances and tribal communities
• 30 major rivers traced from source to sea, with tributaries and the states they flow through
• 18 mountain ranges and hills, and 22 passes and ghats
• All 45 UNESCO World Heritage Sites, including Sarnath (2026)
• All 58 tiger reserves plus key national parks
• Ramsar wetlands, the Tropic of Cancer and the 82.5° E Standard Meridian
• Soil, climate (Köppen) and crop maps
• Highlight states by border country, coastline, Schedule or Zonal Council
• Zoom and pan, with neighbouring countries, seas and a scale bar
• Search that understands old names such as Cauvery, Orissa and Bombay

PRACTISE
• A daily challenge: the same ten questions for everyone each day, with a result you can share with your group
• Eight practice rounds: find the state, capitals, tiger reserves and parks, borders, rivers, passes, UNESCO sites, and ranges and lines
• Wrong answers show the right one on the map, and you get a list of what to revise

BUILT FOR STUDY
• Works fully offline once installed
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• Facts checked against current sources (November 2026)
• The map follows India's official depiction of its boundaries

The map is for study, not survey. Markers and zones are approximate.
