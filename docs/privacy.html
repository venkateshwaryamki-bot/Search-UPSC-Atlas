Privacy Policy

Search UPSC Atlas · Last updated 6 November 2026

Search UPSC Atlas is an offline study app. It does not collect, store or share any personal information.

What the app stores

The app saves a few settings and results only on your own device: which map layers you turned on, your best practice scores and your daily challenge results. This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

What the app does not do

The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos. It makes no network requests while you use it.

Sharing

If you tap "Share result" after a daily challenge, your phone's share sheet opens with a short text of your score. You choose where to send it. The app does not see or keep what you share.

Links to other websites

The app credits its map data sources with links. Tapping a link opens your browser, and that website's own privacy policy applies.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.
